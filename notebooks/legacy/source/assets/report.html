Definitions, provenance & pipeline checks

A late inbound followed by a late departure is an observed association. This report does not establish causal delay propagation or recommend a minimum safe turnaround.
How the aircraft links are built
  • Order all records with an assigned aircraft by scheduled departure. Keep cancelled, diverted and invalid records in that sequence so they can break links.
  • Reconstruct actual departure from scheduled departure plus reported departure delay; reconstruct arrival using actual elapsed time. Cross-check against reported local clocks and arrival delay.
  • Reject ambiguous or nonexistent local times, airport discontinuities, chronological overlaps, and gaps beyond the configured limit.
  • Turnaround recovery = inbound arrival delay − outbound departure delay. Positive values mean fewer delay minutes at the next departure.
  • Records cover reporting U.S. domestic operations, not a complete global aircraft movement log. The first observed leg has no inbound link.

Reproduce this report

The accompanying notebook downloads the public BTS reporting-carrier ZIP files, stores immutable raw copies, builds validated Parquet partitions and a DuckDB warehouse, and exports this report. Replaying the same partition does not duplicate flights. The bundled demo is an explicitly labeled real-data slice.
